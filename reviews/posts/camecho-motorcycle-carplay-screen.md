I added the CAMECHO screen to my bike to get navigation, music, and a dash cam without mounting my phone to the bars. Installing it was easy, and since then it has worked like a charm. It does exactly what it promises.

My one complaint is the wiring. I wish CAMECHO had included a longer cable. With what's in the box, you're limited in how you route it, and a cleaner, hidden install takes some planning or an extension.

## Who it's for

Riders who want CarPlay and a dash cam on their motorcycle without a complicated setup. Plan your wire route before you start.
