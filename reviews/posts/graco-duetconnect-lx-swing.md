When you've got a newborn at home, a good swing is worth a lot, and the Graco DuetConnect LX mostly earns its spot in our house. Once it's set up, it does what a swing should do, and it's been a reliable place to set the baby down for a few minutes.

## Assembly

Getting there is the hard part. Putting the legs on is more complicated than I expected, mainly because the wiring runs through tight spaces where the legs connect. It's easy to pinch or rip a wire if you're not careful, and that's not what you want to deal with on a product that has to work. Take your time on this step and route the wires carefully before you push anything into place.

## The mobile

My other gripe is the toy mobile. I assumed the toys would spin to keep the baby's attention, but they just hang there. It's a small thing, but if you're counting on the mobile for entertainment, know that going in.

## Bottom line

4 stars. It's a good swing that does its main job well, held back by a cramped, wire-snagging assembly and a mobile that doesn't move.
