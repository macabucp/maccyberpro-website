I put the Kojem Class 3 hitch on my Toyota Highlander, and I'm happy with it. It gives me a standard 2-inch receiver for towing and hitch accessories, and the black powder coat blends right in under the rear bumper.

## The one hiccup

The bolts that came in the box weren't the right size, so I had to order ones with the correct threads before I could finish. It didn't change how I feel about the hitch itself, but it did add a delay. My advice is to test-fit the bolts as soon as it arrives so you're not stuck mid-install.

## Bottom line

5 stars. It's a solid hitch that does the job. Just confirm the bolt threads before you start.
