I bought the Soundcore P30i as an everyday pair of earbuds, and they've checked every box. The sound is full and punchy for budget earbuds. The noise canceling quiets things down nicely, and they're comfortable to wear and easy to carry around.

You can pay a lot more for earbuds, but these cover the basics so well that it's hard to justify spending more for daily use.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants solid noise-canceling earbuds for the gym, commuting, or around the house without paying premium prices.
